# Masabu Google İşletme kapağı

Üretim: 10 Eylül 2026, built-in image_gen. Resmî Masabu wordmark dosyası referans alındı. Görseldeki metin ve yerleşim gözle kontrol edildi. Yükleme ve Google onayı henüz yapılmadı.

## Üretim komutu

Use case: ads-marketing / compositing.
Create one finished landscape Google Business Profile cover image for Masabu, a Turkish cafe and restaurant software brand. Target 1600x900 pixels, 16:9, opaque PNG, restrained minimal background so file stays below 5 MB. This is a new cover layout compositing the provided existing wordmark, not a redesign of the logo.
Input image: official masabu wordmark, supporting insert. Preserve its spelling, QR-like black texture on 'masa', green texture on 'bu', and period exactly; remove only its white rectangular backing into the warm cream background without distortion.
Design: exquisite editorial typography, warm off-white #FCF9F4 background, dark ink #171A18, forest green #237451. Spacious, confident, polished SaaS identity. Tiny pale sage and pale sand curved shapes only at far outer corners; thin elegant sage curves suggest connected operations. No photographs, fake product screens, device mockups, Google logo, fake QR codes, gradients, shadows, CTA buttons or promotional badges.
Centered composition, all essential content comfortably inside the central 65% width and 65% height, generous outer margins to survive cropping. Wordmark centered at upper middle, modest but recognizable, roughly 28% canvas width. Below it a large elegant high-contrast serif headline, dark ink, exact Turkish text:
"QR menüyle başla."
Then a smaller highly readable clean sans-serif subtitle in two balanced lines, exact text:
"Sipariş, adisyon ve mutfağı"
"aynı sistemden yönet."
Below a modest small website line:
"masabu.co"
Text must be perfectly rendered with Turkish dotted/dotless i, ü, ı, ğ and ş. No other words. Hierarchy headline dominates, logo secondary, subtitle readable at thumbnail size. No geographic regions, installation promises, pricing, free trial offers, personal information. Produce a real ready-to-upload raster cover, no presentation frame.
